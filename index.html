Frequently asked questions

How do I use it?
Click the mixer icon in the menu bar. Every app that is currently playing sound gets its own slider. Drag a slider to change that app's volume, or click its speaker icon to mute it. The slider at the top controls your output device.

Why does it ask for Screen & System Audio Recording?
macOS only lets an app adjust another app's sound through this permission. Audio is processed live on your Mac to apply your volume settings. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the app never captures your screen.

An app doesn't show up in the list.
Apps appear only while they are playing audio. Start playback in that app and it will show up.

I see “Audio access required”.
Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Per-App Volume Mixer, then quit and reopen the app.

How do I quit it?
Right-click (or Control-click) the menu bar icon and choose Quit.
