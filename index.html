Volume for each browser tab

Chrome plays all of its tabs through one app, so on its own the mixer can only change Chrome's volume as a whole. The free Tab Volume extension adds a slider and a mute button for every tab that plays sound.

  1. Open Per-App Volume Mixer – Tab Volume in the Chrome Web Store. You can also click “Set each tab’s volume: get the extension” under Chrome in the mixer.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Keep Per-App Volume Mixer running and play sound in a tab. Within a few seconds the tab appears under Chrome in the mixer, with its own slider.
  4. If a tab was already playing before you installed the extension, reload that tab once.

The extension also works in Microsoft Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, and other Chromium browsers that can install from the Chrome Web Store. Volume and mute are remembered for each website. The extension talks only to the mixer on your own Mac and sends nothing over the internet.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use it?
Click the mixer icon in the menu bar. Every app that is currently playing sound gets its own slider. Drag a slider to change that app's volume, or click its speaker icon to mute it. The slider at the top controls your output device.

Why does it ask for Screen & System Audio Recording?
macOS only lets an app adjust another app's sound through this permission. Audio is processed live on your Mac to apply your volume settings. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the app never captures your screen.

My browser tabs don't show up.
Install the Tab Volume extension (see above), keep the mixer running, and reload the tab. Tabs appear only while they play sound.

An app doesn't show up in the list.
Apps appear only while they are playing audio. Start playback in that app and it will show up.

I see “Audio access required”.
Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Per-App Volume Mixer, then quit and reopen the app.

How do I quit it?
Right-click (or Control-click) the menu bar icon and choose Quit.
