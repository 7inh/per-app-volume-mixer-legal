Privacy Policy

Per-App Volume Mixer · Last updated November 10, 2026

Summary

Per-App Volume Mixer does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. This includes its companion browser extension, Per-App Volume Mixer – Tab Volume. Everything happens on your Mac.

System audio access

To change the volume of individual apps, the app uses the macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission to route the sound of apps that are playing audio through its own mixer. The audio is processed in real time and immediately played back to your output device at the volume you choose. It is never recorded, saved to disk, analyzed, or transmitted. The app does not capture your screen.

Data stored on your Mac

The app remembers the volume, mute state, and output device you chose for each app, identified by the app's bundle identifier, so your settings survive a restart. This data stays in the app's local preferences and is removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

If your output device disconnects, the app may show a local notification. Notifications are generated on your Mac and are not sent through any server.

Tab Volume browser extension

The optional Per-App Volume Mixer – Tab Volume extension for Chrome and other Chromium browsers lets the mixer set the volume of each browser tab.

  • What it reads: the title, website address, and icon of tabs that are playing audio, so the mixer can show them.
  • Where it goes: only to Per-App Volume Mixer on the same Mac, over a connection to 127.0.0.1 (your own computer). This information never leaves your Mac and is not sent to any server, including ours.
  • What it stores: the volume and mute setting you choose for each website, in your browser's local extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.
  • Page access: to change the volume, the extension adjusts the audio and video elements of the pages you visit. It does not read, collect, or change page content, form data, passwords, or browsing history.

No network, no tracking

The app connects to no server. Its only connection is a local one on your own Mac (127.0.0.1), which accepts only the Tab Volume extension. Neither the app nor the extension contains analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. There are no user accounts.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
